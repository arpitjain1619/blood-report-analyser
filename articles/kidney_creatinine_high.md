# High Creatinine (Kidney Function Marker)

Creatinine is a waste product filtered out of the blood by the kidneys. Higher-than-normal levels generally suggest the kidneys may be filtering less efficiently than usual, though many factors can influence a single reading.

**Common general factors linked to high creatinine:**
- Dehydration
- Reduced kidney filtering function
- High-protein diet or intense recent exercise (temporary effect)
- Certain medications

**General notes:**
Because creatinine can shift with hydration and muscle activity, doctors usually look at trends over time and alongside other kidney markers (like BUN) rather than a single value in isolation.

**Important:** This information is educational only and not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a licensed doctor to interpret your blood report and decide on any next steps.
