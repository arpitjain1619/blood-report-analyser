# Low TSH (Possible Overactive Thyroid)

When TSH is lower than normal, it often means the thyroid is producing more hormone than the body currently needs, causing the pituitary gland to reduce its signal — a pattern generally associated with an overactive thyroid (hyperthyroidism).

**Common general symptoms people report:**
- Unexplained weight loss
- Rapid heartbeat
- Feeling anxious or jittery
- Difficulty tolerating heat

**General notes:**
As with high TSH, a full thyroid picture usually requires additional tests and clinical evaluation, not just one number.

**Important:** This information is educational only and not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a licensed doctor to interpret your blood report and decide on any next steps.
