# Low HDL Cholesterol

HDL is often nicknamed "good" cholesterol because it helps carry excess cholesterol away from the bloodstream. Lower-than-normal HDL is generally associated with a somewhat higher cardiovascular risk profile, though it's usually considered alongside LDL and triglycerides rather than alone.

**Common general factors linked to low HDL:**
- Sedentary lifestyle
- Smoking
- Being significantly overweight
- Genetics

**General lifestyle notes (educational, not prescriptive):**
Regular physical activity and avoiding smoking are commonly cited general factors associated with healthier HDL levels, but individual circumstances vary widely.

**Important:** This information is educational only and not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a licensed doctor to interpret your blood report and decide on any next steps.
