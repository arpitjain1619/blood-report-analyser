# Low Vitamin B12

Vitamin B12 is important for nerve function and red blood cell production. It's found mainly in animal-based foods, so certain diets and absorption issues can lead to lower levels.

**Common general factors linked to low B12:**
- Diets low in animal products (e.g., vegan or vegetarian diets without supplementation)
- Certain digestive conditions affecting absorption
- Some medications
- Age-related absorption changes

**Common general symptoms people report:**
- Fatigue
- Tingling in hands or feet
- Difficulty concentrating

**General notes:**
B12 deficiency is often addressed through diet or supplementation, but the right approach depends on the underlying cause.

**Important:** This information is educational only and not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a licensed doctor to interpret your blood report and decide on any next steps.
