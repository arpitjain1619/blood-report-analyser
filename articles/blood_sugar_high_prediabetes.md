# High Blood Sugar (Fasting Glucose / HbA1c)

Fasting glucose measures blood sugar at a single point in time, while HbA1c reflects average blood sugar levels over roughly the past three months. Higher-than-normal readings are generally associated with reduced insulin sensitivity or the body's ability to regulate blood sugar.

**Common general factors linked to elevated blood sugar:**
- Diet high in refined carbohydrates and sugar
- Low physical activity
- Family history / genetics
- Being significantly overweight

**General lifestyle notes (educational, not prescriptive):**
Regular physical activity, reducing refined sugar and carbohydrate intake, and maintaining a healthy weight are commonly discussed general factors related to blood sugar management — but the right approach should be personalized with a healthcare provider, especially since elevated readings can range from mild to requiring closer monitoring.

**Important:** This information is educational only and not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a licensed doctor to interpret your blood report and decide on any next steps.
