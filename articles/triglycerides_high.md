# High Triglycerides

Triglycerides are a type of fat in the blood, largely influenced by diet and how the body processes sugar and fat. Elevated levels are generally associated with cardiovascular and metabolic health considerations.

**Common general factors linked to high triglycerides:**
- Diet high in sugar or refined carbohydrates
- Excess alcohol intake
- Low physical activity
- Being significantly overweight
- Underlying metabolic conditions

**General lifestyle notes (educational, not prescriptive):**
Reducing added sugars and refined carbs, moderating alcohol, and regular physical activity are commonly discussed general factors — but a personalized approach depends on the full health picture.

**Important:** This information is educational only and not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a licensed doctor to interpret your blood report and decide on any next steps.
