# High TSH (Possible Underactive Thyroid)

TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone) is produced by the pituitary gland to signal the thyroid to produce hormones. When TSH is higher than normal, it often means the thyroid isn't producing enough hormone on its own, so the body is "asking" it to work harder — a pattern generally associated with an underactive thyroid (hypothyroidism).

**Common general symptoms people report:**
- Fatigue
- Weight gain
- Feeling cold more easily
- Dry skin, hair thinning

**General notes:**
Thyroid conditions are usually confirmed with additional tests (like free T3/T4) rather than TSH alone, since TSH can fluctuate for various reasons.

**Important:** This information is educational only and not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a licensed doctor to interpret your blood report and decide on any next steps.
