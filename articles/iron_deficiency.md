# Low Iron

Iron is essential for producing hemoglobin, the part of red blood cells that carries oxygen. Low iron levels are one of the most common causes of low hemoglobin (anemia).

**Common general factors linked to low iron:**
- Diet low in iron-rich foods
- Blood loss (e.g., heavy periods, digestive issues)
- Increased needs (e.g., during growth or pregnancy)
- Poor absorption due to certain digestive conditions

**General lifestyle notes (educational, not prescriptive):**
Iron-rich foods (leafy greens, legumes, lean meats) and pairing them with vitamin C sources are commonly discussed general dietary factors, though the underlying cause of low iron should be evaluated by a doctor.

**Important:** This information is educational only and not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a licensed doctor to interpret your blood report and decide on any next steps.
