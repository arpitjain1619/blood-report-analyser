# Sodium & Potassium Imbalance (Electrolytes)

Sodium and potassium are electrolytes that help regulate fluid balance, nerve signals, and muscle function, including the heart. Because of their role in heart rhythm, abnormal levels — especially potassium — are generally considered more important to flag for prompt medical attention compared to many other biomarkers.

**Common general factors linked to abnormal sodium:**
- Dehydration or overhydration
- Excess fluid loss (e.g., sweating, illness)
- Certain medications

**Common general factors linked to abnormal potassium:**
- Kidney function changes
- Certain medications
- Dehydration
- Dietary factors

**General notes:**
Because both electrolytes are closely tied to heart and nerve function, results outside the normal range — particularly for potassium — are usually treated as more time-sensitive by doctors compared to many other routine blood markers.

**Important:** This information is educational only and not a medical diagnosis. Because electrolyte imbalances can be significant, please consult a licensed doctor promptly to interpret your blood report and decide on any next steps.
