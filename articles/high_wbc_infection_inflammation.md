# High White Blood Cell (WBC) Count

White blood cells are part of the immune system, helping the body fight infections and respond to inflammation. A higher-than-normal WBC count often simply means the immune system is actively responding to something.

**Common general factors linked to high WBC:**
- An active infection (bacterial or viral)
- Inflammation from an injury or illness
- Physical or emotional stress
- Certain medications

**General notes:**
A single high WBC reading is common and often temporary — for example, right after an infection or even intense exercise. It becomes more meaningful when looked at alongside symptoms and other test results, which is why lab values are usually interpreted together rather than one at a time.

**Important:** A high WBC count by itself does not indicate a specific illness. This information is educational only and not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a licensed doctor to interpret your blood report and decide on any next steps.
