# High Liver Enzymes (ALT / AST)

ALT and AST are enzymes found mainly in liver cells. When liver cells are stressed or irritated, these enzymes can leak into the bloodstream in higher amounts, so elevated levels are generally thought of as a general marker of liver stress rather than a specific diagnosis.

**Common general factors linked to elevated liver enzymes:**
- Alcohol consumption
- Certain medications or supplements
- Viral infections affecting the liver
- Fatty liver-related factors (often linked to diet and weight)

**General notes:**
A single elevated reading is fairly common and can be temporary. It's usually interpreted alongside other liver-related tests and a person's overall health history.

**Important:** Elevated liver enzymes can have many causes, ranging from minor and temporary to more significant. This information is educational only and not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a licensed doctor to interpret your blood report and decide on any next steps.
